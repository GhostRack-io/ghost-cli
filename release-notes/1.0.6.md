- **CPU level of images:** `ghost catalog images` shows each image's CPU level (`x86-64-v3`): the least x86-64 level a host's CPU needs to run it. Rocky Linux 10 and CentOS Stream 10 need `x86-64-v3` (AVX2), so offerings whose hosts lack it do not take them; AlmaLinux 10 runs on every host, as a build for the host's CPU.
