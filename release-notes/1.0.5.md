- **Deposit lookup:** `ghost deposit lookup <txid>` shows what became of a payment, one line per output: its amount, its state (credited, pending, returning, returned, kept) and its return. It needs no account, so a deposit sent without its memo can be looked up too.
- **Terms:** `ghost account activation-terms` closes with the Terms of Service line that sending a deposit and activating agree to; `ghost account activate` itself prints nothing of the kind.
- **Includes 1.0.4**, which was not published on its own: payouts in MMX (`ghost provider payout --amount` takes MMX with at most six decimals, and the backend refuses `--amount-microusd` from ghost 1.0.3 and earlier), the MMX balance views and `ghost provider conversions`.
