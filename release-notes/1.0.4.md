- **Payouts in MMX:** earnings convert to MMX once a day after their hold ends, at that day's exchange rate, and a payout pays MMX. `ghost provider payout --amount` takes MMX with at most six decimals, such as `40.5`; `--amount-microusd` is gone, and the backend refuses it from ghost 1.0.3 and earlier.
- **Balance:** `ghost provider balance` and `ghost account balance` show pending earnings in USD and what is available in MMX, and say when the conversion waits for an exchange rate.
- **Conversions:** `ghost provider conversions` lists each day's conversion: the earnings, the rate and the MMX, newest first. `ghost provider payouts` shows what each payout pays in MMX.
